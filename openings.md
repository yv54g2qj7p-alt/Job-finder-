## 2. Per-brand notes (visa / working rights and eligibility)

None of the search snippets mentioned **visa sponsorship**, either offered or refused. Where a snippet stated an eligibility condition, it is quoted or paraphrased below. Anything else is marked "not stated in search results". Check the full posting before applying.

- **BMW (Munich):** Internships list "at least a bachelor's-level design degree" and fluency in design tools. The working-student role's listings disagree on whether it needs bachelor's or master's level. Visa / right-to-work: not stated in search results. *General context, not from the postings:* German *Praktikum* and *Werkstudent* roles usually require current university enrolment.
- **Audi (Ingolstadt / US):** Interior internship aimed at enrolled students. The US Design Loft role asks for an industrial/automotive design background with sketching and 3D (aggregator summary). Work authorisation: not stated in search results.
- **Porsche (Weissach):** Styling internship requires at least 3 semesters in automotive / transportation / product / CMF / graphic design. The Exterior Design – Porsche Style internship requires **German and English**. Visa: not stated.
- **Mercedes-AMG (Affalterbach):** Internships listed for a Sep 2026 start. Eligibility and visa: not stated in search results.
- **Lamborghini (Sant'Agata):** The exterior design internship requires **Master's-level enrolment or recent graduation** in transportation design, **fluent English**, and ideally **Italian and German**. Visa: not stated.
- **Ferrari (Maranello):** Design Academy is for **Master-level** graduates or students in vehicle / transportation / industrial / interior / engineering design who **graduated within the last 12 months** (or are graduating that year). Portfolio required. Selection runs assessment → recruiter interview → design challenge → final interviews in Maranello. Visa: not stated.
- **Maserati (Turin):** The Junior Exterior Creative Designer posting asks for a **BAC+5** (5-year degree, roughly master's level) in fine arts or industrial/product/automotive design. The listing contradicts itself on experience, saying both 0–2 and 3–5 years. Visa: not stated.
- **Bugatti Rimac (Berlin):** Visa / right-to-work not stated in search results. One aggregator listed an unverified salary (~$90k).
- **McLaren (Woking):** The Visualisation Assistant role asks for a degree in automotive or 3D product design plus VRED and Adobe. UK right-to-work: not stated in search results.
- **Gordon Murray Automotive (Shalford):** The posting states **"all applicants must have the Right to Work in the UK"**. No mention of sponsorship.
- **Aston Martin (Gaydon):** Right-to-work: not stated in search results.
- **Koenigsegg (Ängelholm):** Visa: not stated. Most design-titled ads have closed. Current open ads are engineering roles: Mechanical Design Engineer (deadline 14 Aug 2026), Vehicle Systems (deadline 22 Sep 2026) and Interior & Safety Design Engineer (deadline 9 Sep 2026).
- **Italdesign (Moncalieri):** Aimed at **new graduates**. "A portfolio link on the résumé is essential." Visa: not stated.
- **Pininfarina:** Intern roles are in Cambiano. The Miami senior role needs 5+ years. Visa: not stated.
